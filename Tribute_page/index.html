Dr. Norman Borlaug

Dr. Norman Borlaug, the Father of the Green Revolution, is credited with saving billions from starvation.

Tribute Information

Dr. Norman Borlaug was an American agronomist and humanitarian whose work in developing high-yielding varieties of wheat helped alleviate hunger and poverty worldwide. He is often referred to as the "Father of the Green Revolution" for his role in increasing agricultural production in developing countries.

Achievements

  • Father of the Green Revolution
  • Nobel Peace Prize winner (1970)
  • Presidential Medal of Freedom recipient

Quotes

"There are no miracles in agricultural production."

- Dr. Norman Borlaug
