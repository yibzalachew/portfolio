Who Am I?

I am Yibzalachew Seifu, a Computer Science graduate from Hawassa University, Ethiopia, with a strong interest in software development and technology.

I focus on full-stack web development, creating responsive websites and practical web applications using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, PHP, and MySQL.

These projects have helped me strengthen my skills in frontend development, backend programming, database management, and problem-solving.

I am continuously learning, improving my technical abilities, and looking for opportunities to contribute to meaningful software projects while growing as a developer.
