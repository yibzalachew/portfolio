Guitar Types and Features

At Original Guitar, we offer a variety of guitars, each with unique features to fit your musical needs. Check out the details of our most popular guitar types:

1. Acoustic Guitar
  • Tonewoods: Typically made from solid spruce tops and mahogany or rosewood backs and sides for a rich, warm tone.
  • Versatility: Great for solo performances, songwriting, or jamming with friends
  • Popular Models: Dreadnought, Concert, Parlor.
  • Sound: Rich, natural sound that resonates with the body of the guitar.
  • Perfect For: Folk, country, rock, and classical music.
2. Electric Guitar
  • Pickups: Equipped with single-coil or humbucker pickups that convert string vibrations into electrical signals for amplification.
  • Body Style: Available in solid body (like the Fender Stratocaster), semi-hollow, and hollow body designs.
  • Sound: Powerful and flexible tones, from clean and crisp to overdriven and distorted.
  • Popular Models: Fender Stratocaster, Gibson Les Paul, Ibanez RG.
  • Perfect For: Rock, metal, blues, jazz, and experimental genres.
3. Bass Guitar
  • Strings: Typically four strings (though five- and six-string versions are available) tuned lower than the standard guitar.
  • Neck: Longer neck and scale length, designed to provide deep, resonant bass frequencies.
  • Sound: Powerful, punchy, and low-end heavy.
  • Popular Models: Fender Precision Bass, Fender Jazz Bass, Gibson Thunderbird.
  • Perfect For: Rock, funk, jazz, blues, and any genre requiring a solid rhythm section.
